Frequently asked questions

What documents are required for ITR filing? +

The exact documents depend on your income sources and return type. Send your requirement through the inquiry form and we'll share the checklist relevant to your case.

What is the difference between Regular, Revised and Updated Return? +

A Regular Return is filed within the original due date. A Revised Return corrects a Regular Return filed before the deadline. An Updated Return is filed after the deadline, within the extended window the law allows. We'll confirm which applies to your situation.

How can I send my requirement? +

Use the inquiry form on this page, or contact us directly on WhatsApp or by phone.

Do you provide GST and TDS services? +

Yes — GST registration and return filing, and TDS return filing across 24Q, 26Q, 27Q and 26QB, along with TDS corrections.
